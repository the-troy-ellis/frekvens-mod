5 — Before you close the case

  • Green on OUT. The two jacks are identical from the outside. If the chain runs but panel 1 stays dark, this is the first thing to check.
  • Band the UPDI wire. White is unambiguous on its own header, but PA0 is the one net where a mistake costs you the ability to reprogram the chip.
  • Fit the 10 kΩ before first power-up. Without it the drivers can light power-up garbage for 60+ ms while the rail is still climbing.
  • Never burn SYSCFG0 = 0xC8. It encodes RSTPINCFG = RESET and disables UPDI; recovery then needs 12 V HV programming. Field chips read 0xC5.
  • Flash from the Arduino IDE sketch (arduino/frekvens_panel/). The PlatformIO build for this part has never produced a working binary.
  • PA2 stays unconnected. The panel is write-only — MISO has nowhere to go.
